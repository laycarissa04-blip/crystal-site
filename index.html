Crystal Fambrini

Meet Crystal Real EstateMediaWeb3LongevitySocial Impact

Star clients include celebrities, CEOs and creatives who value an intimate first class experience.

In addition to real estate, Crystal is an established broadcaster and known talent herself. She is a well-known multi-EMMY Award-winning TV host and executive producer, Oscar-nominated documentarian and journalist, established signed commercial model and impactful do good entrepreneur raising millions for various philanthropic initiatives.

Crystal grew up being mentored by her father, a successful Northern California property investor and contractor and started managing her family’s income properties and tenants while attending college where she also worked as an on air news anchor.

Read full bio

Crystal enjoys finding the best homes and income properties for her star roster. From starter homes to world class estates, she works with her clients to source and secure the best deals in California as well as works with her network of realtors throughout the world to get what is needed done – always with a smile.

Her experience with flipping homes, property management, and events allows her to step in and support her clients in a variety of areas. In 2025, Crystal purchased and renovated a home in the Los Angeles area in anticipation for the demand in housing for artists due to upcoming events such as World Cup, Super Bowl, and Olympics. In 2024, she put an A lister’s home in escrow day 1 on the market, establishing herself as a top realtor in America and coverage of the deal can be seen in The Real Deal. She also successfully flipped a house in Northern California for record breaking profit, redid a multi million dollar house in The Hollywood Hills, found and secured several deals for her clients throughout the county of Los Angeles, continued to manage estates in the 8 figures and yes of course she is a 5 star super host.

Crystal is a licensed realtor in the state of California and is a member of the Malibu Association of Realtors.
